Ravella Ultra ingredients

Ingredients and amounts as listed by the maker. Always read the label on your bottle.

  1. 50%

    Alkaline water, pH 8.5

    The base of the drink. It keeps Ravella Ultra light and easy to take, and adds to your daily hydration.

  2. 20%

    Noni root (Morinda citrifolia)

    The main herb. Noni has been used across the tropics for centuries to support energy, stamina and the body's natural defences.

  3. 10%

    Neem (Azadirachta indica)

    Known in Nigeria as dogoyaro. Neem is a traditional cleansing herb, valued for supporting general internal wellness.

  4. 10%

    Senna (Senna alata)

    Also called candle bush or asunwon oyinbo. Senna is used in herbal tradition for internal cleansing and as a natural laxative.

  5. 10%

    Papaya (Carica papaya)

    Pawpaw is traditionally used to support digestion, and contains natural plant antioxidants.

How to take it

  1. Shake the bottle well.
  2. Take 30 ml after a meal, once in the morning and once in the evening.
  3. Wait about 10 minutes, then drink a glass of warm water.

Don't take more than 60 ml a day. If you notice an unusual reaction, stop and speak to a healthcare professional.

Who it isn't for

Ravella Ultra is for adults only. It isn't recommended for:

  • children
  • pregnant or breastfeeding women
  • anyone allergic to an ingredient listed above

If you have a medical condition or take medication, check with your doctor first.

Ravella Ultra questions and answers

What is Ravella Ultra?

Ravella Ultra Solution Organic Wine is a sugar-free, non-alcoholic herbal wellness drink made in Nigeria. It contains noni root, neem, senna and papaya extracts in pH 8.5 alkaline water. Each bottle holds 750 ml.

What is Ravella Ultra used for?

Adults take Ravella Ultra as part of a daily wellness routine. People use it to support energy and stamina, comfortable digestion, internal cleansing and the body's natural defences. It's a wellness drink, not a medicine.

What are the benefits of Ravella Ultra?

The main benefits people look for are steady energy through the day, support for digestion and regularity, internal cleansing and hydration. It's also a sugar-free, alcohol-free swap for sweet drinks. These benefits come from the traditional use of its herbs and from customer experience.

Is there alcohol in Ravella Ultra?

No. “Organic wine” is part of the product name. The drink is completely alcohol-free and sugar-free.

Is Ravella Ultra NAFDAC approved?

Ravella Ultra is NAFDAC listed under registration number A7-103154L. The “L” means it is a listed herbal product that NAFDAC has assessed for safety. Check that this number is printed on your bottle.

How do I take Ravella Ultra?

Shake the bottle well. Take 30 ml after a meal in the morning and again in the evening. About 10 minutes later, drink a glass of warm water. Don't take more than 60 ml a day.

How long does one bottle last?

At 30 ml twice a day, a 750 ml bottle lasts about 12 days. A month's supply is about 3 bottles.

Does Ravella Ultra have side effects?

Ravella Ultra contains senna, a natural laxative, so some people notice looser stools or more frequent bowel movements. Stick to the recommended amount. If you have an unusual reaction, stop taking it and speak to a healthcare professional.

Can people with diabetes or high blood pressure take Ravella Ultra?

Ravella Ultra is sugar-free, but it isn't a treatment for diabetes, high blood pressure or any other condition. If you have a medical condition or take medication, ask your doctor before taking it. Don't stop or reduce your prescribed medicine.

Who should not take Ravella Ultra?

Children, pregnant women and breastfeeding mothers shouldn't take it. Neither should anyone allergic to noni, neem, senna or papaya. If you have a medical condition, check with your doctor first.
